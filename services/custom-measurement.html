Custom Window Measurement

There is no such thing as a standard window in an existing home. Our custom measurement protocol eliminates guesswork, ensuring your new Vitrava windows fit snugly with optimal expansion gaps.

PROVEN ADVANTAGES

Engineered Performance You Can Measure

Sub-Millimeter Accuracy

Class II digital laser rangefinders eliminating tape sag and human transcription error.

3-Point Opening Survey

Measuring width and height across top, middle, and bottom to detect out-of-square wall framing.

Wall Cavity Depth Verification

Analyzing interior drywall return depths and exterior siding reveals for perfect flush aesthetics.

Structural Header Inspection

Verifying load-bearing header integrity and sill plate soundness prior to custom factory fabrication.

EXECUTION PROTOCOL

How We Execute This Service

Step 01: Opening Preparation

Removing interior stops or trim covers where necessary to access the true rough frame framing.

Step 02: Laser Triangulation

Capturing exact width, height, and cross-diagonal dimensions to check for wall settlement or racking.

Step 03: Profile & Casing CAD Record

Recording exact sill slope angle and casing reveal depth into our mobile architectural specification tablet.

Step 04: Factory Order Verification

Direct digital transmission to Vitrava automated manufacturing lines with zero transcription loss.
