THERMAL TECHNOLOGY

Why Vitrava Frames Never Warp, Rot, or Weaken

Conventional vinyl windows warp under direct solar heat, while wood frames rot from moisture. Vitrava windows utilize a proprietary structural composite blend of thermoplastic polymer and reclaimed wood fibers.

  • 2x Stronger than Vinyl: Allows significantly slimmer frame profiles with expansive glass.
  • Thermal Expansion Matching: Expands and contracts at the exact same rate as glass, protecting seals.
  • Zero Repainting: Color is factory-extruded through the material core; will not peel or flake.
