Modern Transformation Lake Oswego, OR

Contemporary Casement Transformation

Replacing dated 1980s cloudy aluminum sliding windows with sleek, modern architectural casements featuring trimless drywall returns and high-flow summer ventilation.

Installation Scope:
14 Outward Casement Units with Drywall Return Trim
Vitrava Signature Casement with Charcoal Grey Frames
Book Measurement Request Similar Quote
EXISTING CONDITIONS

The Challenge & Thermal Deficit

The existing aluminum sliders had failed perimeter seals, cloudy foggy glass, and required considerable physical muscle to push open along corroded tracks.

Pre-Installation Audit Findings:

  • • Extreme temperature differential between center-glass and frame edges
  • • Severe convective drafts near seating areas and bedroom beds
  • • Deteriorated exterior perimeter caulk allowing moisture into wall cavity
VITRAVA ENGINEERING

The Architectural Solution

Installed 14 custom-engineered Vitrava Casement Windows with smooth 4:1 rotogear cranks, multi-point concealed deadbolts, and European shadowline drywall return trims.

Engineered Installation Highlights:

  • • AAMA 2400 certified continuous low-expansion micro-foam air seal
  • • Rot-proof synthetic composite shims preventing settlement
  • • 25-Year non-prorated transferable warranty issued upon signoff
