The 5-Step Homeowner Preparation Checklist

Vitrava master installation teams handle all heavy lifting, surgical removal, and job-site vacuuming. However, completing a few simple preparatory steps before our arrival ensures your project proceeds seamlessly and safely:

1. Clear Window Access Pathways

Our technicians will be carrying heavy triple-pane window units into each room. Please clear a 5-foot perimeter around all window openings, removing floor lamps, potted plants, small side tables, and fragile decorative objects.

2. Remove Window Treatments & Blinds

Take down all curtains, drapes, sheers, and interior blinds prior to installation morning. This protects delicate fabrics from construction dust and gives our installers full access to the window casing.

3. Deactivate Window Security Sensors

If your home features a hardwired or wireless security alarm system connected to window frames or magnetic contacts, notify your alarm monitoring provider and disarm the sensors before work begins.

4. Secure Household Pets

With exterior doors frequently opening and unfamiliar technicians working throughout the home, pets can easily become startled. We recommend keeping cats and dogs comfortable in an unworked room or arranging daycare.

5. Clear Exterior Landscaping Access

Trim back any thorny bushes, heavy tree branches, or dense vines growing directly against exterior window frames to allow our crew safe ladder placement and clean perimeter caulking.

Key Takeaways for Homeowners

  • Always demand certified NFRC independent ratings rather than relying solely on manufacturer claims.
  • In Northern climates, prioritize a low U-Factor (≤ 0.22) and tight air infiltration (< 0.02 CFM/ft²).
  • Professional installation with continuous closed-cell foam and sloped sill pans is essential for lasting performance.
